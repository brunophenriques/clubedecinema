# Chrome edition: visual research

Eleven actual reference images were inspected in three local contact boards. Public Pinterest search returned a login shell; image search and original portfolios provided the references without authentication. Reference artwork is not used in the website.

## Inspected references

1. [Halftone cover](https://ar.pinterest.com/pin/882916701960782074/): tightly cropped photography, compact edition numbers, strong ink contrast.
2. [2000s music captions](https://www.pinterest.com/pin/200s--715439090839070631/): pink caption blocks and deliberately varied image scales.
3. [Pink collage](https://www.pinterest.com/pin/14073817579204960/): controlled overlaps; useful as a layering study rather than a template for decoration.
4. [Revista Y2K](https://www.behance.net/gallery/217592015/Revista-Y2K): blue halftone photography and small information blocks beneath oversized lettering.
5. [Feel the Bliss](https://www.behance.net/gallery/164247609/Feel-The-Bliss-(Y2K-Magazine)): full-page photography, contrasting title scale, compact margins.
6. [Year Zero](https://www.behance.net/gallery/113275935/Year-Zero-Y2K-Zine): digital-window framing and silver/blue accents.
7. [QUA CHUA KAY](https://www.behance.net/gallery/171835549/QUA-CHUA-KAY-Y2KS-Magazine): pale pink editorial panels and metallic display lettering.
8. [Pinknoise lettering](https://www.behance.net/gallery/194958233/Y2K-CHROME-LOGO-FREE-FONT): dark outlines, reflective highlights and a coherent wordmark silhouette.
9. [Hyperpix lettering](https://www.behance.net/gallery/155913919/Y2K-CHROME-LOGO-TEXT-EFFECT-PSD): sharp highlight bands that distinguish chrome from a smooth gradient.
10. [Mystery Clock Cinema, original portfolio](https://www.polaine.com/2000/11/mystery-clock-cinema-website/): film imagery organized as a publication, compact captions and asymmetric scale.
11. [Photography-led spread](https://www.pinterest.com/pin/224124518948169428/): white space around dominant photography with small pink/blue captions.

## Implemented vocabulary

Original CSS metal lettering is reserved for the masthead. Ordinary controls retain readable solid text. Paper, black and pink remain the foundation; icy blue identifies edition information, contact sheets and window headers. Large film photography stays unfiltered. Numbered selection frames alternate a small pink number field; film posters retain their proportions and alignment. Club averages use bold pink printed labels. Archive details form numbered contact sheets. The existing chat becomes a messenger window with silver bevels and pink/blue messages, preserving its controls and real messages.

No artificial cover stories, fabricated stills, decorative fake window controls or invented member counts are introduced. The winner remains the backend's assigned winner. Ties, pending decisions and no-vote weeks use compact factual notices and the real selection. Reduced-width layouts retain touch controls and keyboard focus indicators. Browser verification uses intercepted API fixtures for states that do not exist in the local database; no fixture data is shipped.
